What we do

Six things, done well, rather than a menu as long as your arm.

Nails, lashes and brows all happen in the same room, so you can get more than one thing sorted in a single visit instead of chasing appointments around town.

01

Nail Extensions

Built to the length and shape you actually want, sculpted so they sit flush at the cuticle and hold their shape through a full working roster.

02

Manicures

A proper tidy up, cuticle work and colour that survives the dishes, the gym and a ski glove. Kids' manicures are welcome too.

03

Pedicures

Feet sorted before sandal season, or put back together after a season in ski boots. Soak, file, cuticles and colour.

04

Bridal Nails

Nails for the wedding, the hens and every photo in between. A trial set first, so there are no surprises on the morning.

05

Lash Lift & Tint

Your own lashes lifted and tinted so you wake up ready. Nothing to maintain daily, no mascara, no glue to worry about.

06

Brow Lamination

Brows brushed up, set and shaped to sit where you want them, then tinted to suit your colouring rather than a chart.

The studio

A calm room and an appointment that is yours.

AK Studio sits on Portree Drive in Lower Shotover, run by Antoneta. It is a private studio rather than a salon floor, so there is no queue behind you and no clock being watched while your top coat cures.

Most people come in for one thing and leave with three sorted, because nails, lashes and brows all happen in the same room. Bookings go through a phone call, an email or an Instagram message, whichever is easiest for you.

Nails, lashes and brows, all in the one room.

Everything below is done at the studio on Portree Drive. If you are not sure what you need, call and describe it. You will get a recommendation rather than a sales pitch.

Nail Extensions

Acrylic or gel extensions sculpted to the length and shape that suits your hands, finished flush at the cuticle so the regrowth line stays tidy. Infills booked before the set has a chance to lift.

Manicures

Shape, cuticle work, buff and colour on your natural nails. A good option if you want something that looks tidy for work without committing to extensions. Kids' manicures welcome.

Pedicures

Soak, hard skin, nails cut and shaped, cuticles tidied, then colour if you want it. Worth booking after a season in ski boots or before a summer of jandals.

Bridal Nails

A trial set well ahead of the day so the colour, shape and length are settled, then the real thing close to the wedding. Bridesmaids can be booked in the same block.

Lash Lift & Tint

Your own lashes curled up from the root and tinted darker, so they read as done without mascara. Nothing glued on, nothing to fill, and it suits people who swim or ski regularly.

Brow Lamination & Shaping

Brows brushed up and set into the shape you want, tidied at the edges and tinted to match your hair rather than a standard chart. Good for filling gaps without pencil.

Give the studio a call and get booked in.

Weekday appointments run through to 7pm, and the studio is open Saturday and Sunday too.

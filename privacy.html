Privacy Policy

What AK Studio collects when you get in touch, and what happens to it.

Placeholder wording for this demo site. Antoneta, review and adjust this to match how the studio actually handles client details before the site goes live.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers AK Studio, Nails & Beauty Queenstown, at 30 Portree Drive, Lower Shotover, Queenstown 9371. It explains how personal information is handled under the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What is collected

There is no contact form on this website, so nothing is collected simply by browsing it. Information is only collected when you choose to get in touch or book an appointment.

  • Your name and the contact details you use to reach the studio
  • The services you book and the dates of your appointments
  • Anything you tell the studio about allergies or skin sensitivities, so treatments can be carried out safely
  • Photographs of completed work, where you have agreed to them

3. How it is used

Your details are used to confirm bookings, to contact you about an appointment, and to carry out your treatment safely. They are not sold, rented or traded.

4. Who it is shared with

Client details are not shared with third parties for marketing. Information may be held within the everyday tools the studio uses to run bookings and email, and may be disclosed where the law requires it.

5. How long it is kept

Booking and treatment notes are kept for as long as they are needed to look after you as a client, then deleted. The studio's retention period will be set out in this section.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Pages embed a Google Map, and Google may set its own cookies through that embed under Google's privacy policy.

7. Photographs and social media

Photographs of finished work may be posted to the studio's social media accounts. Tell your technician if you would rather your work was not posted, and it will not be.

8. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information the studio holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Get in touch using the details below and the studio will respond as soon as it reasonably can.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can go to the studio on 021 226 6273, or by email.
